## 3DEXPERIENCE Platform

**What it is:** The overarching business platform that unifies Dassault Systemes' applications, including
DELMIA, BIOVIA, ENOVIA, CATIA, and SIMULIA, on a single data model. It is not a product in the traditional
sense; it is the connective layer.

**Core value proposition:** Breaks down silos between R&D, manufacturing, quality, and supply chain by
giving every function access to the same product and process data in real time, rather than reconciling
data across disconnected systems after the fact.

**Best-fit trigger signals:** New CIO or Chief Digital Officer hires, company-wide digital transformation
programs, mergers and acquisitions that require integrating two organizations' systems, and any account
explicitly talking about "unifying data" or "breaking down silos."

**Typical buyer:** Chief Digital Officer, Chief Information Officer, VP of Digital Transformation, or a
Chief Technology Officer sponsoring an enterprise-wide initiative.

## DELMIA

**What it is:** The manufacturing operations brand within 3DEXPERIENCE, covering production planning,
manufacturing execution (MES), plant and process simulation, and the digital twin of the factory.

**Core value proposition:** Lets a manufacturer plan and simulate a plant before it is built, then run and
monitor production against that same digital model once it is live, so new sites come online faster and
existing sites run more predictably.

**Best-fit trigger signals:** New plant announcements, capacity expansion CAPEX, multiple concurrent site
build-outs, named cost or footprint optimization programs, supply chain disruption, and any signal
mentioning MES or plant digitization directly.

**Typical buyer:** VP or Head of Manufacturing Operations, VP of Global Engineering, Plant Director, or
Head of Manufacturing Technology.

## BIOVIA

**What it is:** The scientific informatics brand, covering laboratory information management (LIMS),
formulation and process development, electronic lab notebooks, and quality and regulatory data management
for scientific processes.

**Core value proposition:** Connects the science — R&D, formulation, and quality — so that process
knowledge developed in the lab carries through to manufacturing without being re-created, which matters
most in biologics and cell and gene therapy where the process is the product.

**Best-fit trigger signals:** Biologics or ADC manufacturing investment, cell and gene therapy
manufacturing challenges or delays, quality or compliance system changes, formulation and process
development news, and lab informatics mentions.

**Typical buyer:** Head of R&D, VP of Quality, Head of Process Development, or Chief Scientific Officer.

## MEDIDATA

**What it is:** Dassault Systemes' clinical trials technology brand, covering clinical data capture, trial
management, and patient-centric clinical operations software.

**Core value proposition:** Speeds up clinical trials and improves data quality across the trial lifecycle,
which is most relevant when an account's news centers on clinical trial results, trial delays, or
regulatory submissions rather than manufacturing.

**Best-fit trigger signals:** Clinical trial news, PDUFA dates and regulatory submission activity, and any
account signal about accelerating time to market for a pipeline asset. Use as a secondary mention when the
primary signal is manufacturing-led.

**Typical buyer:** Head of Clinical Operations, VP of Clinical Data Management.

## ENOVIA

**What it is:** The collaborative product lifecycle management (PLM) and data governance brand, managing
product data, change processes, and compliance records across an organization.

**Core value proposition:** Provides the governed system of record for product and process data, most
relevant when an account is talking about data governance, regulatory submission data management, or
integrating systems after an acquisition.

**Best-fit trigger signals:** Post-acquisition systems integration, regulatory data governance initiatives,
and references to data domain governance such as product, batch, equipment, or quality data.

**Typical buyer:** Head of Quality, Director of Data Governance, VP of Regulatory Affairs.

## How to use this reference

When writing a Strategic Recommendation, match the account's signal to the brand whose "best-fit trigger
signals" it matches most closely, and write the rationale using that brand's actual value proposition
rather than a generic pitch. If a signal touches more than one brand (for example, a biologics plant build
touches both DELMIA and BIOVIA), lead with the primary brand and mention the secondary one as a follow-on
conversation.
