## Essence

- Core identity: "Virtual Worlds for Real Life"
- Brand promise: Unify scientific innovation and manufacturing excellence on a single collaborative
  platform.
- Brand narrative: Dassault Systèmes empowers life sciences and manufacturing leaders to transform how
  therapies and products are discovered, developed, manufactured, and delivered. Through scientifically
  accurate virtual worlds, industrial AI, and virtual twin experiences, the 3DEXPERIENCE platform creates a
  unified digital thread connecting R&D, quality, manufacturing, and patient outcomes.

## Voice pillars

- **Scientific authority** — precise, evidence-based, technically confident.
- **Operational excellence** — performance-driven, manufacturing-ready, reliability-focused.
- **Human impact** — outcome-oriented, patient-centric, empathetic.
- **Sustainability & harmony** — visionary, responsible, future-focused.
- **Platform unification** — integrative, collaborative, ecosystem-centric.

## Tone characteristics

- Confident but not boastful
- Technical but not academic
- Human-centered but not sentimental
- Visionary but not vague
- Operationally grounded but not conceptual

## Sentence structure

- Short declarative sentences for clarity; longer technical sentences when needed.
- Minimal filler words.
- **No exclamation marks. No double hyphens.**

## Perspective

- First-person plural for brand voice ("we").
- Second-person when addressing customers ("you").
- Avoid passive voice unless describing scientific or manufacturing processes.

## Vocabulary

**Core terms:** Virtual Twin, Scientific AI, Modeling, Simulation, Data Intelligence, Unified Platform,
Digital Thread, Predictive, Sustainable, Patient-Centric, GxP-Ready, Manufacturing Excellence, Quality by
Design, End-to-End Continuity, Single Source of Truth.

**Preferred phrases:** "Virtual Worlds for Real Life," "From Molecule to Manufacturing," "Unified
Scientific and Manufacturing Platform," "Outcome-Driven Innovation," "Sustainable Value Networks,"
"Integrated R&D to Manufacturing," "Predictive and Compliant Operations."

**Avoid:** generic digital buzzwords, overly emotional language, fragmented brand references, excessive
jargon without context.

## Messaging frameworks

- **Platform master message:** The 3DEXPERIENCE platform unifies scientific discovery, product
  development, and manufacturing execution in a single collaborative environment.
- **Life sciences master message:** We help life sciences organizations accelerate discovery, improve
  development, and optimize manufacturing through scientifically accurate virtual twins and purpose-built
  scientific AI.
- **Manufacturing master message:** We enable manufacturers to design, simulate, and optimize products and
  processes with predictive intelligence and digital continuity.

**Sub-brand integration:**
- BIOVIA: "Scientific innovation from molecule to manufacturing."
- DELMIA: "Manufacturing excellence and operational performance."
- MEDIDATA: "Clinical intelligence and patient-centric outcomes."
- All sub-brands speak as one platform.

## AI enforcement rules

**Deterministic requirements:** use the vocabulary system; anchor messaging to the unified platform;
connect scientific and manufacturing concepts; tie technology to human or product outcomes; maintain tone
and structure rules.

**Prohibited output:** double hyphens, exclamation marks, fragmented brand narratives, generic digital
transformation language, overly emotional phrasing.

**Required contextual anchors:** scientific accuracy, operational precision, human impact, sustainability,
unified platform.
