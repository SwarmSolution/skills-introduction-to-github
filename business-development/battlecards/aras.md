# Battlecard: 3DEXPERIENCE / ENOVIA vs. Aras Innovator

**Competitor:** Aras Corporation — Aras Innovator (PLM platform)
**3DS side:** ENOVIA (collaborative PLM and data governance brand within 3DEXPERIENCE)
**Status:** General-purpose battlecard. No confirmed incumbency at any target account as of 2026-07-24 —
use this for objection-handling and proactive positioning, not as an account-specific competitive callout
until we have a confirmed signal.

## Snapshot

Aras positions itself as a "low-code" and "open and adaptable" PLM and digital thread platform, built on a
graph-based data model that customers (or their systems integrators) configure extensively rather than
deploy out of the box. Its signature commercial hook is a subscription-only, no-perpetual-license model with
a free Community Edition of the core platform, positioned against the traditional up-front license plus
maintenance model used by most enterprise PLM vendors. Aras has real enterprise reference accounts
(Airbus, Microsoft, GM, and others cited by the vendor and by CIMdata, which has ranked Aras among its PLM
Mindshare Leaders) and, since its 2022 acquisition of implementation partner Minerva Group, a more built-out
medical device and high-tech electronics vertical offering aimed at FDA-regulated design history file and
compliance workflows. It remains a much smaller company than Dassault Systèmes, Siemens, or PTC, with a
narrower partner ecosystem and less brand recognition outside the PLM community itself.

## Where they win

- **Zero up-front license cost and a genuinely different commercial model.** Aras Innovator's core platform
  is distributed as a free Community Edition, with revenue coming from subscription support and premium
  modules. For a budget-constrained IT or engineering organization, this reframes the buying conversation
  from a capital software purchase to an ongoing services relationship, which can be an easier initial
  approval to get, especially for a first PLM deployment or a point project.
- **Upgrade-safe customization architecture.** Aras stores customizations as metadata rather than compiled
  code, and markets this as the reason customers can upgrade to new platform versions without re-validating
  every workflow they built. In regulated environments where re-validation after every system change is
  expensive and slow, this is a genuine and frequently cited differentiator, and it directly targets the
  pain that heavily customized legacy PLM deployments (including some ENOVIA and Windchill deployments)
  create at upgrade time.
- **Deep, low-code configurability.** Aras's graph-based object/relationship model lets a customer or
  integrator model business objects, workflows, and data relationships that don't map cleanly to a vendor's
  pre-built application structure. This appeals to engineering-led IT organizations that want to own their
  data model and build highly specific, non-standard processes rather than adapt to a vendor's prescribed
  application.
- **Enterprise-scale references and improving analyst standing.** Aras has landed large, credible logos
  (Airbus at roughly 30,000 seats, GM at roughly 50,000 seats, Microsoft) and CIMdata has moved Aras into
  its top PLM Mindshare Leaders category. It is not a niche or unproven vendor at this point, and reps
  should not treat it as one.
- **A specific, built-out medical device / regulated manufacturing story since the Minerva acquisition.**
  Aras's 2022 acquisition of Minerva Group brought a purpose-built medical device PLM offering aimed at FDA
  design history file (DHF) and compliance workflows, plus reference customers such as Dräger for EDHF
  digitization. This means Aras can now credibly speak to medtech quality and regulatory buyers rather than
  relying solely on the generic platform pitch — worth taking seriously if it surfaces in a medtech deal.

## Where 3DS wins

- **Single unified platform, not a framework you build a PLM on top of.** ENOVIA is one governed brand
  inside 3DEXPERIENCE, sharing a common data model with CATIA, SIMULIA, DELMIA, and BIOVIA out of the box.
  Aras gives a customer a highly configurable platform and largely leaves them (or their integrator) to
  build the connective tissue to design, simulation, manufacturing, and quality applications. For an
  account already running or evaluating CATIA, SIMULIA, or BIOVIA, ENOVIA's native shared data model is a
  materially different proposition than integrating those domains onto an Aras data model from scratch.
- **Single source of truth without a lengthy configuration project.** Our core differentiator — governed
  product, process, and quality data on one platform, accessible in real time across R&D, quality,
  manufacturing, and supply chain — is largely native to 3DEXPERIENCE. Aras's "low-code" model achieves a
  comparable outcome only after a substantial modeling and configuration effort, which shifts cost and risk
  from license fees to implementation and integration services.
- **Purpose-built regulated life sciences and medtech depth across the full value chain.** BIOVIA connects
  lab, formulation, and process development data through to manufacturing; MEDIDATA covers clinical trials;
  DELMIA covers plant and MES; ENOVIA governs product, batch, equipment, and quality data across all of it.
  Aras's regulated-industry strength, even post-Minerva, is concentrated in medical device engineering
  change and DHF workflows — it does not have an equivalent to BIOVIA's lab-to-manufacturing connection or
  MEDIDATA's clinical data platform. For accounts spanning biologics, cell and gene therapy, or clinical-to-
  commercial continuity, that breadth is a real gap on the Aras side.
- **Lower implementation and validation risk for accounts that want a configured application, not a
  platform to build.** Aras's flexibility is also its cost: multiple independent reviews and analyst
  commentary cite lengthy, expert-dependent implementations, difficult migrations from other systems, and a
  requirement for ongoing specialist support to manage the platform's complexity. 3DEXPERIENCE's
  pre-built, GxP-ready applications reduce the amount of from-scratch configuration a regulated customer
  needs to validate before go-live.
- **Ecosystem and partner depth.** Aras's partner network and out-of-the-box module depth are both
  materially smaller than the Dassault Systèmes ecosystem, per third-party comparisons. For an account that
  wants system integrator choice, breadth of pre-built industry solutions, and a large installed base to
  draw reference implementations from, that matters.
- **Cloud-native SMB and mid-market path.** 3DEXPERIENCE Works gives smaller or fast-growing regulated
  manufacturers a cloud-native, subscription-based entry point without the multi-year configuration project
  that a from-scratch Aras deployment typically implies, addressing the same "avoid up-front capital
  commitment" motivation Aras uses as its hook, but with less implementation lift.

## Common objections and responses

**"Aras is free — why would we pay Dassault Systèmes license fees for the same thing?"**
We would separate the platform from the outcome. Aras's Community Edition removes license cost for the
core platform, but a governed, validated PLM deployment connected to design, manufacturing, and quality
still requires significant configuration, integration, and validation work, and that work is where most of
the real cost and risk sits over the life of the deployment. With 3DEXPERIENCE, you are paying for a
platform where ENOVIA, CATIA, SIMULIA, DELMIA, and BIOVIA already share one data model, so a meaningful
share of that integration work is already done before your project starts. We would ask what the account's
total cost of ownership looks like across a five-year horizon, not just at signature.

**"Aras lets us customize the platform to our exact process without waiting on a vendor roadmap."**
That flexibility is real, and for a narrow, one-off business process it can be the right call. But when we
look at accounts running discovery-through-manufacturing programs — where product data, batch records,
equipment data, and quality data all need to stay connected — that same flexibility means the account owns
the burden of building and validating those connections themselves, and re-validating them as the business
changes. We would ask how many of those connections the account actually wants to build and maintain versus
inherit from a platform that already unifies them.

**"Aras's low-code architecture means our customizations survive upgrades — we've been burned by
expensive PLM re-validation before."**
That is a legitimate pain point, and it is worth taking seriously rather than dismissing. We would point to
3DEXPERIENCE's cloud-delivered releases and configuration-over-customization approach as our answer to the
same problem: the more of an account's process lives in native 3DEXPERIENCE applications rather than deep
custom code, the less re-validation burden they carry at upgrade time. We would ask what specifically drove
past upgrade pain, since the answer is often heavy customization of an older on-premises deployment rather
than something inherent to the platform.

**"Aras has landed major accounts like Airbus and GM — they're not a toy vendor anymore."**
Agreed, and we would not suggest otherwise to a customer who raises this. Aras has real enterprise scale in
discrete manufacturing and has earned recognition from CIMdata. Our answer is not that Aras cannot scale —
it is that scale in discrete engineering PLM does not translate directly into the regulated life sciences
and medtech breadth this account needs, where the connection between lab, clinical, manufacturing, and
quality data is the actual value driver, not just PLM seat count.

## Landmines to avoid

- Do not claim Aras is "unproven" or "not enterprise-grade." It has credible large-scale references
  (Airbus, GM, Microsoft) and improving analyst standing. Making this claim to a customer who has done their
  own diligence will cost us credibility.
- Do not claim Aras has "no regulated industry story." Since the 2022 Minerva acquisition, Aras has a
  specific medical device PLM offering built around FDA design history file and compliance workflows, with
  named customers (Dräger). The accurate differentiator is breadth across the full life sciences value
  chain (lab, clinical, manufacturing, quality), not the absence of a medtech offering.
- Do not overstate ENOVIA's out-of-the-box simplicity relative to Aras without qualification. 3DEXPERIENCE
  deployments, especially validated GxP configurations, still require real implementation effort. The honest
  claim is that more of the cross-domain integration work is done natively than with Aras, not that our
  deployments are turnkey.
- Do not lean on license-cost comparisons alone. Aras's "free platform" positioning is a marketing framing
  as much as a technical fact — real deployments carry substantial subscription, services, and integration
  cost — but 3DS reps should compete on total cost of ownership and outcome, not by disputing the free
  license claim itself, which is accurate as far as it goes.
- Be careful with "single vendor, single data model" claims in accounts that already run significant non-3DS
  toolchains (e.g., a different CAD or MES system). The unified-platform advantage is strongest when CATIA,
  SIMULIA, DELMIA, or BIOVIA are already in play or realistically on the roadmap; in an account with an
  entrenched non-3DS design or manufacturing stack, Aras's openness and willingness to integrate with
  third-party tools is a genuine competing strength, not a bluff.
