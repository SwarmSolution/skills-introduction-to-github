# Battlecard: Siemens (NX / Teamcenter / Simcenter / Opcenter / Dotmatics) vs. Dassault Systèmes 3DEXPERIENCE (DELMIA / BIOVIA)

**Scope:** Primary focus on DELMIA (manufacturing operations, digital twin of the factory) and BIOVIA
(scientific informatics) exposure to Siemens' life-sciences and manufacturing stack. Secondary notes on
NX/CATIA and Simcenter/SIMULIA included where relevant to a broader platform conversation.

**Drop-in use:** Written to paste directly into an account's Account Accelerator "Competitive Landscape &
Risks" section (Key Competitors & Threats, ≤100 words, plus supporting detail below for account team
reference).

**Last updated:** 2026-07-24

---

## Key Competitors & Threats (Account Accelerator summary, ~100 words)

Siemens is the most credible full-stack competitor to 3DEXPERIENCE in life sciences manufacturing.
Teamcenter/Opcenter/Simcenter now carry a direct digital-twin proof point (PepsiCo: over 90% of
operational issues caught before physical implementation, 20% throughput gain) that DELMIA prospects will
hear about. The $5.1B Dotmatics acquisition, combined with the new Luma Agent (agentic R&D co-scientist)
and Digital Twin Composer / Intelligence Center X, gives Siemens a genuine BIOVIA-adjacent scientific
informatics plus digital twin story for the first time. Where Siemens is still exposed: Dotmatics is a
recent, unproven integration; Teamcenter's data model is more federated than ENOVIA's single source of
truth; and Siemens' MOM stack is stronger in discrete/process manufacturing than in the biologics- and
GxP-specific workflows BIOVIA was purpose-built for.

---

## Where They Win

- **Digital twin credibility with a named, quantified reference.** Digital Twin Composer (GA mid-2026,
  built on Siemens' own digital twin stack plus NVIDIA Omniverse libraries) and Intelligence Center X
  (industrial AI orchestration layer announced at Realize LIVE Americas 2026) are backed by PepsiCo's
  public numbers: over 90% of potential operational issues caught before physical build, 20% throughput
  improvement across the end-to-end value chain. This is the single most useful competitive proof point
  Siemens reps will lead with against DELMIA, and it is real, not vaporware. (Sources: Siemens/PepsiCo
  press release, Siemens CES 2026 announcement, Tecnomatix blog.)
- **Life-sciences-specific manufacturing execution.** Opcenter Execution Pharma is a dedicated,
  purpose-built MES for pharma/life sciences with paperless manufacturing and electronic batch recording
  (EBR); Opcenter Execution Medical Device is built for the traceability, closed-loop quality, and IoMT/
  personalized-medicine requirements of medtech. Gartner has called Siemens the strongest of the "big
  three" PLM developers specifically on the MOM/MES side. This is a legitimate, well-established DELMIA
  competitor in plants that are further along in MES maturity.
- **Newly credible scientific informatics + agentic AI story via Dotmatics.** The Dotmatics acquisition
  ($5.1B, closed 2025) gives Siemens R&D data integration, ELN/LIMS-adjacent capability, and Luma Agent —
  an agentic AI "co-scientist" built on structured scientific data with a traceability architecture Siemens
  is positioning for regulated lab environments. Siemens is explicitly messaging "molecule to
  manufacturing" (their words) by pairing Dotmatics with Simcenter simulation and digital twins, claiming
  therapies could reach patients "up to 50% faster and at a lower cost" (Siemens claim, unverified —
  treat as vendor marketing, not independent benchmark). This is the first time Siemens has had a
  plausible answer to BIOVIA's core pitch.
- **Multi-CAD and legacy manufacturing depth.** Teamcenter has genuine strength in complex, multi-CAD
  manufacturing environments (not just NX) and carries deep production-process integration from its
  on-premise heritage — a real advantage in accounts with heterogeneous CAD environments from M&A.
- **NX for MCAD/ECAD-heavy medtech.** NX's tool-centric, engineer-controlled workflow integrates
  mechanical, electrical, and electronics design tightly within Teamcenter — attractive to medtech
  engineering teams building connected/electromechanical devices who want less process overhead than a
  platform-centric CATIA/3DEXPERIENCE deployment.
- **Installed base and price/seat economics on the CAD side.** NX has a larger raw customer/seat count
  than the 3DEXPERIENCE platform in CAD software market-share data, and incumbency in existing Siemens
  accounts (Teamcenter PLM already in place) creates real switching-cost inertia.

## Where 3DS Wins

- **Unified platform vs. federated stack, especially post-acquisition.** 3DEXPERIENCE runs DELMIA, BIOVIA,
  ENOVIA, CATIA, and SIMULIA on a single data model. Siemens' equivalent story is a set of separately
  acquired and engineered products (Teamcenter, Opcenter, Simcenter, and now Dotmatics) being stitched
  together under the Xcelerator umbrella and the new Intelligence Center X orchestration layer. Dotmatics
  in particular is a brand-new (2025) acquisition; Siemens itself frames "integration challenges" as the
  primary risk to realizing the deal's value, and analysts note technology-compatibility and
  business-process alignment could delay synergies. For an account weighing "buy one platform" against
  "buy a Siemens Xcelerator + freshly acquired Dotmatics" story, 3DS can credibly claim the data model was
  unified from the outset rather than integrated after the fact.
- **ENOVIA as true single source of truth vs. Teamcenter's federated model.** Teamcenter carries more
  architectural legacy from its on-premise origins and, per independent PLM comparisons, is more prone to
  performance issues on large datasets and complex workflows when not carefully optimized. ENOVIA's
  advantage is being native to the same platform CATIA, DELMIA, BIOVIA, and SIMULIA already run on —
  compelling for accounts that want to stop reconciling product, process, batch, and quality data across
  systems after the fact, which is exactly the pitch DELMIA and BIOVIA are built to make.
- **BIOVIA's purpose-built depth in biologics and cell/gene therapy, versus Dotmatics' breadth-first
  approach.** BIOVIA was purpose-built around the idea that "the process is the product" in biologics and
  cell/gene therapy — connecting lab, formulation, and quality data through to manufacturing. Dotmatics'
  strength is broad R&D data integration and Luma Agent's agentic capability; it does not yet have BIOVIA's
  track record connecting scientific process knowledge directly into GxP manufacturing execution. This is
  a real, defensible depth argument, not just an incumbency argument.
- **DELMIA's manufacturing-to-execution continuity on the same platform as design and quality.** DELMIA's
  differentiator against Digital Twin Composer/Intelligence Center X is not "who has a digital twin" (both
  do) but that DELMIA's digital twin sits on the same platform and data model as CATIA design data, ENOVIA
  change/compliance records, and BIOVIA process data — so a plant simulation is built on the same product
  and process definition used upstream, not a re-derived model stitched together across Teamcenter,
  Opcenter, and a third-party AI orchestration layer.
- **Cloud-native SMB and mid-market motion.** 3DEXPERIENCE Works and the 3DExperience for Startups program
  (multi-year, up to 90% first-year discount) give 3DS a genuinely cloud-native, lower-friction entry point
  for smaller medtech and biotech manufacturers that Siemens' more enterprise/on-prem-legacy Teamcenter
  deployment model does not match as cleanly.
- **Breadth of simulation domains under one house.** SIMULIA covers structural mechanics, thermal, fluid,
  and electromagnetics (via Abaqus and CST Studio Suite) with model-to-result traceability inside the same
  platform as design and manufacturing data — a genuine "designed, simulated, and built in one place"
  story that Simcenter, strong as it is standalone, still delivers through a more separately-licensed set
  of tools.

## Common Objections and Responses

**"Siemens already proved 90%+ issue avoidance and 20% throughput gains with PepsiCo — what can you show
that's comparable?"**
We do not dispute that result, and we would rather you hear it from us than be surprised by it later.
What we would point out is what sits underneath that number: a digital twin built from data spread across
Teamcenter, Plant Simulation, and a newly launched product, Digital Twin Composer. Our digital twin of the
factory in DELMIA is built on the same product and process definition your engineers are already
authoring in CATIA and governing in ENOVIA, so what you are simulating is what you actually designed and
approved to build, not a separately reconstructed model. We are glad to scope a comparable pilot on your
own plant data rather than ask you to take a packaged-goods case study on faith for a regulated
manufacturing environment.

**"Siemens just bought Dotmatics, so now they have R&D data and manufacturing in one Xcelerator story,
just like you."**
Owning both is not the same as having built them together. Dotmatics closed in 2025, and Siemens' own
public risk framing acknowledges that merging Dotmatics' scientific software with its industrial systems
requires careful coordination and could delay the value they are promising customers. BIOVIA was built
from the start to carry process knowledge from the lab through formulation, quality, and manufacturing on
the same data model DELMIA runs on. If your organization is choosing a platform to build the next decade
of biologics or cell and gene therapy manufacturing on, we would ask what happens to your data model while
two companies' engineering teams spend the next several years reconciling two different architectures.

**"We already run Teamcenter and NX — why would we add another PLM/CAD platform instead of extending what
we have?"**
We are not asking you to rip out a working environment on day one. Where we typically start is a specific
gap: a plant digitization program, a biologics or ADC manufacturing build-out, or a quality and compliance
initiative where your existing Teamcenter deployment does not natively carry lab and process data through
to the shop floor. From there we can talk about how DELMIA or BIOVIA plugs into that specific need, and
what a longer-term platform consolidation could look like once we have shown value on a bounded scope.

**"Gartner rates Siemens strongest in MOM/MES — Opcenter is more mature than DELMIA for us."**
For discrete and general process manufacturing, that is a fair characterization of the market, and we
would not tell you otherwise. Where the comparison changes is in biologics, cell and gene therapy, and
other GxP-intensive environments, where the harder problem is not running the shop floor but keeping
process knowledge, batch records, and quality data connected to what R&D actually developed. That is
where BIOVIA and DELMIA working off the same platform data model earn their keep, and it is a narrower
but more consequential slice of your operation than general MES throughput.

**"Your platform has a steeper learning curve than NX or Teamcenter."**
That is a fair characterization for teams new to the platform, and we would rather be upfront about it
than have you discover it after go-live. CATIA and 3DEXPERIENCE are process-driven by design, because
that discipline is what lets design, manufacturing, and quality data stay connected without manual
reconciliation later. We would rather scope a phased rollout with your team than undersell the ramp-up.

## Landmines to Avoid

- **Do not claim DELMIA has a PepsiCo-scale, publicly quantified digital-twin reference of its own.** We
  are not aware of a comparable, named, numbers-backed 3DS case study at this scale in the market right
  now. Do not imply one exists or gesture vaguely at "similar results" without a specific, sourced
  account. If pressed for a number, offer to scope a pilot rather than invent a comparable figure.
- **Do not overclaim ENOVIA is trivially easy to deploy or cheap at scale.** Independent comparisons
  consistently flag ENOVIA/3DEXPERIENCE integration with non-DS enterprise systems (e.g., SAP) as complex
  and often requiring middleware and experienced integration partners, and migrations from legacy ENOVIA
  V5/V6 to 3DEXPERIENCE involve real data-model and customization rework. If a prospect has heavy SAP or
  legacy ENOVIA dependency, flag the integration lift honestly rather than promise a frictionless
  migration.
- **Do not dismiss Dotmatics/Luma Agent as vaporware.** Luma Agent is a real, shipped product (launched
  by Dotmatics, now under Siemens) built specifically for agentic scientific workflows with a traceability
  architecture aimed at regulated environments. Competing on architecture and track record, not on
  pretending the product does not exist, is the credible line.
- **Do not repeat Siemens' "50% faster to patients, lower cost" Dotmatics claim as if it were verified.**
  That figure comes from Siemens' own marketing around the Dotmatics/Simcenter combination and has not
  been independently verified in the sources reviewed for this battlecard. If a prospect raises it, it is
  fair to note it is a vendor projection, not an audited result, and ask what specific proof point they
  were given.
- **Do not claim across-the-board simulation superiority.** SIMULIA and Simcenter are closely matched on
  breadth of multiphysics coverage; Simcenter STAR-CCM+ in particular has genuine strengths in unified
  pre/solve/post workflows. Win the conversation on platform integration (simulation on the same data as
  design and manufacturing) rather than on solver-for-solver superiority claims we cannot support.
- **Do not overstate CAD market share.** By seat count and market share in CAD software specifically, NX
  has a larger installed base than the 3DEXPERIENCE platform. Lead with platform integration and
  data-model unification value, not raw CAD market-share claims, when NX is the incumbent.
- **Do not treat "strongest in MOM/MES" (Gartner) as contestable in general manufacturing.** That
  assessment is Siemens' to hold in broad discrete/process MES maturity. Narrow the battle to GxP,
  biologics, and cell/gene therapy workflows where BIOVIA/DELMIA's platform-native process continuity is
  the differentiator, rather than arguing DELMIA is more mature MES in general.
